Daily Journal Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

In short: Daily Journal does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Your entries and photos are stored only on your device. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

This policy explains how the Daily Journal app for Android (package com.nikhil.journal, "the app") handles your information. The app is developed by Nikhileshwar ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store on our servers, share or sell any personal or usage data. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send information off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not use a sign-in or account system.

Information stored on your device

Everything you add to the app stays on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Journal entries: titles, text and dates you enter.
  • Photos you attach: when you add a photo, the app saves a resized copy in its private storage so the entry keeps working even if you delete the original from your gallery.

Other apps cannot access this data, and we cannot access it either.

Photos and camera

  • Gallery: photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker. The app can see only the photos you select, not the rest of your library, and does not request storage permissions.
  • Camera: taking a photo opens your device's camera app. Daily Journal does not request the camera permission and only receives the photo you capture.

Backups and moving to a new phone

The app's data is excluded from Google cloud backup, so your journal is never uploaded to the cloud. On Android 12 and later, if you transfer data directly from an old phone to a new one (device-to-device transfer during setup), Android may copy your journal to the new phone. That transfer is done by Android between your own devices; we are not involved and receive nothing.

Deleting your data

  • Delete an entry in the app to remove it and its photos.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, permanently deletes all entries and photos. Because nothing is backed up to the cloud, this cannot be undone.

Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever begins collecting data, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
